Game Dev Blog

Dev logs, progress updates, and behind-the-scenes content from Snowfall and Stuck In Time.

Snowfall • Dec 15, 2024

Dynamic Weather System Implementation

Just finished implementing the core weather system for Snowfall. Particles are now reacting to wind direction, and snow accumulation respects surface angles. The ambient audio system dynamically adjusts based on how heavy the snowfall is. Next up is adding footstep sounds that change based on snow depth...

Stuck In Time • Dec 8, 2024

Rhythm Mechanics: Syncing Puzzles to Music

Experimenting with beat detection and procedural puzzle generation. The goal is to have obstacles spawn and move in perfect sync with the soundtrack. Early prototypes are promising - the time manipulation mechanic feels natural when tied to musical phrases. Working on a custom editor tool to help level designers align puzzles to BPM...

General • Nov 29, 2024

Balancing Music Production and Game Development

A lot of people ask how I manage to produce music while developing games. The truth is they feed into each other. Composing tracks for Stuck In Time has improved my understanding of rhythm design, and coding game systems has made me think about music structure differently. Here's my daily workflow and how I avoid burnout...
